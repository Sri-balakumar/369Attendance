Attendance & Late Tracking

Office hours, how each day is graded, what is deducted, the late and absent lists, and the monthly summary. Every page in the module also opens with a yellow How to use banner listing its steps.

The one rule to remember: being late costs nothing. A Half Day is the only deduction, and an Absent day is simply not earned. Days off and public holidays are always paid.

1. Where to find it

Under Attendances → Attendance Status:

  • Late Records — every late check-in, with its late time and reason.
  • Day Status — one row per employee per day: Present, Late, Half Day, Leave, Absent or Day Off.
  • Absent Today — who was expected today and has not checked in.
  • Monthly Summary — late days and late time per employee for a month.

Under Attendance Status → Configuration: Office Hours & Working Days and Public Holidays.

2. Set the rules — Office Hours & Working Days

Each record holds the rules for a company. Add another record with a Department to give that department different rules; a department record wins over the company one for its own people.

SettingWhat it does
Late TrackingThe master switch. Off: check-ins are still recorded but nobody is marked late.
Require Late ReasonAsks for a reason when a late check-in is entered or edited here in Odoo. Check-ins from the phone app and kiosk are never blocked.
Office TimezoneThe office clock. Every check-in is converted to it before being compared with Office Start. Empty: each employee's own timezone, then UTC.
Grace MinutesHow long after Office Start someone can still arrive on time.
Office Start / EndThe working day, in 24-hour time.
Daily Paid HoursFilled from the office hours; type over it if lunch is unpaid (09:30–18:30 is 9 hours, but 8 may be paid).
Late Window EndsArrive after this and the day shows Present (arrival time in brackets). No check-in by this time on a working day: Absent, stamped automatically. 0 = off.
Half Day AfterArrive after this and the day becomes a Half Day, so half a day's pay is cut. 0 = off.
Half Day Below RatioA fraction of Daily Paid Hours (0.5 = half). Check out having worked less than that share: Half Day. 0 = off.
Working DaysTick the days the company works. Unticked days are never marked Absent and are paid.
Recompute Late Records (button on the form): run it after changing the rules so the last 3 months follow them.

3. How a day is graded and what is deducted

The first check-in of the day is compared with the office clock. The checks run in this order; the first that matches wins:

SituationDay labelPay
Arrived after Half Day AfterHalf DayHalf a day cut
Checked out having worked less than Half Day Below Ratio of the paid hoursHalf DayHalf a day cut
Arrived after Office Start + Grace, but before Late Window EndsLateNothing
Anything else (including arriving after Late Window Ends)PresentNothing
No check-in on a working dayAbsentThe day is not earned
daily rate = Employee Monthly Wage / working days in the month half-day charge = daily rate / 2
Example: start 09:30, grace 15, Late Window Ends 11:00, Half Day After 13:30. In at 09:40 → Present. In at 09:50 → Late (20 min — late time is counted from Office Start, not from the end of grace). In at 11:30 → Present (11:30 AM). In at 14:00 → Half Day.

4. How late detection works

  • The check-in is converted to the Office Timezone.
  • If it is later than Office Start + Grace Minutes, the record is late, with the late time measured from Office Start.
Example (Asia/Muscat): check-in stored 04:20 UTC = 08:20 Muscat; start 08:00, grace 15 → 20 min late.

5. Late Records and late reasons

Menu: Attendance Status → Late Records. The Late Only and Missing Reason filters, and grouping by employee or month, help you see who is often late.

  • Enter Late Reason on a late record opens a popup showing the late time and expected start, then saves the reason without leaving the form. The button hides once the record is checked out.
  • With Require Late Reason on, a late record edited here cannot be saved without a reason.

6. Day Status, Absent Today, Public Holidays and Comp Off

  • Day Status rows appear when someone checks in. Absent rows are added automatically (every 30 minutes) once the office passes Late Window Ends. Open a row and press Recompute after changing the rules.
  • Absent Today lists today's absentees. Someone who checks in later drops off by themselves; approved leave shows as Leave, not Absent.
  • Public Holidays (Configuration): nobody is marked Absent on a holiday, and it is paid. A holiday on a day already off changes nothing.
  • Compensatory Off: working on a day off or a holiday earns a credit when Comp Off is switched on in Leave Policy.

7. Monthly Late Summary

Menu: Attendance Status → Monthly Summary. Choose the Month and Year, optionally a Department or specific Employees, then Generate Summary. Each row shows Total Late Days and Total Late Time (H:MM). Only the first check-in of each day counts, and arrivals after Late Window Ends are not counted. Generating again replaces the previous summary. For pay, use Employee Report → Generate Report.

8. Office-time display

Standard Odoo date columns show the viewer's timezone. The attendance lists/kanban/form, Employee Report and the Late Attendance PDF show dedicated Office-Time values anchored to the Office Timezone, so everyone sees the same office time regardless of their settings. The same Office-Time display also covers Vehicle Tracking (trip Start/End Time) and Customer Visits (Date and Time) — see the HR Field Attendance guide.

9. Help & User Guides

Menu: Help — each guide is a card. Open guide shows it in the browser (this HTML); Open in PDF doc opens the full PDF manual. Admins manage these under Help Documents: edit the HTML content and Upload PDF to replace the bundled manual — an uploaded PDF takes priority over the shipped one.
