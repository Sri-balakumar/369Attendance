What this does

Attendance Suite takes care of everything that happens around a check-in. When someone checks in, it works out what kind of day it was: on time, late, a half day, on leave, absent or a day off. We call that the Day Status. It lets people ask for leave or to work from home, and it tells everyone up front whether a leave will be paid. People who work on a Sunday or a holiday get a day back later, called a compensatory off. At the end of the month, all of this feeds the payslips and the monthly report, so pay always matches attendance.

HR sets the rules once: office hours, holidays, how much paid leave people get, and who approves what. After that the module runs on its own. One idea runs through all of it: coming in late costs nothing, a half day costs half a day's pay, and an absent day is simply not paid. Sundays and public holidays are always paid.

Key words used in this manual

WordWhat it means in plain terms
Working dayA normal workday: one of the ticked weekdays that isn't a public holiday. Only working days can be late, absent or counted as leave.
Day offA weekly off, such as Sunday, or a public holiday. Nobody is ever absent on a day off, and it is always paid.
Daily rateOne day's pay: the Monthly Wage divided by the number of working days that month. Every deduction is "so many days × the daily rate".
Grace minutesA few minutes of allowance. Arrive within them and you are still on time.

Half dayComing in very late, or leaving very early. It is the only status that cuts pay, by half a day.
Not earnedHow an absent day is handled. It isn't subtracted as a penalty; it just isn't counted among the days paid.
Paid leave allowanceHow many paid leave days a person gets per year and per month. Leave is paid only while both still have room.
Loss of Pay (LOP)Days that are not paid: unpaid leave, absent days, and half of each half day.
Compensatory offA day off given back to someone who worked on a Sunday or a holiday. It is kept separate from paid leave.

Auto-approvalIf nobody answers a request within a set time, the system approves it.

A day in the life

This short story shows how the pieces fit together. It uses a 9:30 start with 15 grace minutes, a late window ending at 11:00, and a Monthly Wage of 26,000 in a month with 26 working days, so one day's pay is 1,000.

  1. Monday. Priya checks in from the app at 9:50. That's after 9:45, so the day is Late (20 min). The app asks why, and Priya types "bus was late". Being late costs nothing.
  2. Tuesday. Priya doesn't come in and hasn't asked for leave. At 11:00 the late window closes, and within half an hour Priya is marked Absent. HR sees the name in Absent Today. That day won't be paid: 1,000 less this month.
  3. Wednesday. Priya asks for two days of leave next week. Before sending, the app shows that 1 day is paid and 1 day is unpaid, because this month's paid day is already used. HR approves it. The unpaid day will cost 1,000.
  4. Sunday. There's a stock count, so Priya taps I am working today, checks in at 9:00 and out at 16:00. That earns 1 compensatory off day, to take any time before it expires.
  5. Month end. HR runs payroll. Priya's payslip shows the absent day and the unpaid leave day as Loss of Pay: 2 days, 2,000. The late Monday and the Sunday cost nothing.
Part 1 — Setting Up

Done in Odoo by an HR Manager, once, and again only when a rule changes. If your office hours, holidays and leave policy are already in place, skip to Part 2.

1Where everything lives

Everything is inside the Attendances app. Here is what each menu is for.

Open Attendances from the Odoo main menu. The module adds these menus:

  • Attendance Status — see who was late, what each day was, who is absent today, and a monthly lateness count.
    • Configuration → Office Hours & Working Days and Public Holidays — the rules for a normal workday.
  • Devices — which phone belongs to which employee.
  • Leave Requests — ask for leave, approve it, and see balances and compensatory offs.
    • Configuration → Leave Policy and Auto-Approval — how much paid leave people get, and automatic approval.
  • Work From Home — ask for and approve work-from-home days.
  • Employee Details — which extra details you record about staff, salary parts and ID numbers.
  • Payroll — monthly payroll runs and payslips.
  • Employee Report — the monthly attendance and pay report, with PDF and Excel.
  • Help — user guides like this one. They are managed under Configuration → Help Documents.

Every screen opens with a yellow How to use box that explains it in a few steps. What a person sees depends on their role; see Who can do what in Part 3.

2Office hours and working days

Tell the system when the working day starts and ends, and which days people work. Everything else is judged against this.

Open Attendance Status → Configuration → Office Hours & Working Days. You need one record for the company. Fill in:

  • Late Tracking — the main switch. Turn it off and check-ins are still recorded, but nobody is ever marked late.
  • Require Late Reason — when someone is late, they must say why. In the app, a box pops up right after a late check-in.
  • Company, Department and Office Timezone — who these rules are for, and which clock to use. Leave Department empty for the whole company.
  • Grace Minutes — how many minutes after the start time still count as on time.
  • Office Start and Office End — the working day, for example 09:30 to 18:30.
  • Daily Paid Hours — filled in for you as End minus Start. If lunch isn't paid, lower it, for example to 8 instead of 9.
  • Working Days — tick the days people work. Unticked days are weekly offs: nobody is ever absent then, and they are always paid.

The Day Status Ladder settings handle the in-between cases. Each one is switched off while it is 0.

SettingWhat it does, in plain words
Late Window EndsThe cut-off hour. Anyone who hasn't checked in by then is marked Absent. Anyone who arrives after it is simply Present, with the arrival time shown so it's still visible.

KRA Workday Creates AttendanceOnly if you use the KRA/KPI app. Starting the workday there checks the person in; ending it checks them out.

Different hours for one department. Add a second record and pick the Department. For example, Sales can start at 10:00 while everyone else starts at 9:30. A department record replaces the company record completely for its people, so fill in every field, not just the ones that differ.

Watch out: Late minutes are counted from the start time, not from the end of the grace period. With a 9:30 start and 15 grace minutes, 9:45 is on time, but 9:50 is 20 minutes late, not 5.
Tip: When you save changes here, the last 3 months are re-checked under the new rules automatically. The Recompute Late Records button does the same whenever you want, for example after adding a holiday.
Important: If there is no record at all, the ladder is off. Nobody is ever marked absent or given a half day until you create one.

3Public holidays

List the days the whole company is off, so nobody is marked absent and everyone is paid.

Open Attendance Status → Configuration → Public Holidays. Add one row per holiday with a Holiday Name and a Date. You can type straight into the list, or use the calendar view.

  • Counts — shows whether the holiday falls on a normal workday. Only then does it change the month's working days.
  • Company — a holiday applies to the whole company. You can't set a holiday for one department.
Example: why holidays change the daily rate

March has 26 working days. With a 26,000 wage, one day's pay is 1,000. Add a holiday on a Wednesday and March has 25 working days, so one day's pay becomes 1,040. Everyone still gets 26,000 for a full month, but an absent day or a half day that month costs a little more. A holiday on a Sunday changes nothing, because Sunday was already off.

Good to know: Only one holiday is allowed per date. If two festivals fall on the same day, put both names in one row, for example "Onam / Guru Jayanthi".

Open Leave Requests → Configuration → Leave Policy. Keep one record per company.

Example: the yearly and monthly limits together

The policy gives 12 paid days a year and 1 per month. Priya has used 3 paid days this year, so 9 are left for the year. Priya hasn't taken any leave in June, so 1 is left for June. Priya asks for 2 days in June: 1 is paid, 1 is unpaid. The monthly limit is what decides it. July brings 1 fresh paid day again.

Important: Leave is paid only while both the yearly and the monthly allowance have room. Whatever is left over is unpaid. With no Leave Policy saved at all, every leave is unpaid.
Good to know: The allowance starts fresh each calendar year. Unused paid leave does not carry into the next year. Only compensatory offs carry forward.

5Auto-approval

If a manager forgets to answer a request, the system can approve it for them after a while.

Open Leave Requests → Configuration → Auto-Approval. The same screen is also under Work From Home. Create one record for your company.

  • Auto-approve Leave Requests and Approve after — how long a leave request can wait before it's approved automatically, in minutes, hours or days.
  • The same two settings for Work From Home Requests. You can switch on one, the other, or both.
Example

Leave auto-approval is set to 24 hours. Priya asks for leave on Monday at 10:00, and nobody opens it. Around 10:00 on Tuesday it becomes Approved, with OdooBot shown as the approver and Auto ticked. If the manager rejects it on Monday afternoon, auto-approval never happens.

Good to know: The system checks every 10 minutes, so approval can come up to 10 minutes after the wait. Auto-approval ships switched off. A wait of 0 also means off.

6Devices

Keep a record of which phone belongs to which employee.

Open Devices and click an employee. Type the 10-digit Device ID shown on their phone and set a PIN, then save. Device Name and Last Used fill in by themselves.

  • One phone per person — adding a second phone is refused. Replace the old one instead.
  • Active — switch it off for a lost or replaced phone, instead of deleting it.
Good to know: Devices is a record of who owns which phone. In the current version, it doesn't stop anyone checking in from another phone.

7Employee details

Choose which extra details you keep about staff, how salaries are split, and which ID numbers you record.

The Employee Details menu is for Settings administrators. Everything here starts switched off, so nothing new appears on the employee form until you turn it on.

  • Field Settings — which details you record. Start with the Everyone record and switch on sections: Salary Breakup, Statutory Identifiers, Bank & Payment, Personal & Emergency, and Employment History. Add a Per employee record only for someone who needs something different.
  • Salary Components — the parts of a salary, such as Basic or HRA. Eight ready-made ones start switched off: Basic, HRA, Conveyance, Medical, Special, PF, ESI and Professional Tax. Each is an Earning or a Deduction, and is a fixed amount or a percentage. Drag them into the order they should print on the payslip.
  • Statutory ID Types — the official ID numbers you keep. Seven ready-made ones start switched off: PAN, Aadhaar, UAN, PF Number, ESIC Number, Passport Number and Driving Licence. Required means everyone must have one. Confidential hides it from other staff. A Validation Pattern can check the number is typed in the right format.
Example: splitting a 30,000 salary
ComponentTypeHow it's worked out
BasicEarningFixed 15,000
HRAEarning40% of Basic = 6,000
SpecialEarningFixed 9,000
PFDeduction12% of Basic = 1,800

The earnings add up to 30,000, which matches the Monthly Wage, so payroll can go ahead. The 1,800 PF is taken off to give the net pay.

Important: Employees never see salary. They can fill in their own personal, ID and qualification details from My Profile, but only the fields you switched on here.

8The employee form

A few things the module adds to each employee in the Employees app.

  • Monthly Wage — on the Payroll tab, under Attendance Deductions. Every deduction and payslip starts from this number. If it's 0, nothing is ever deducted.
  • Salary breakup — the salary components for this person. The earnings must add up to the Monthly Wage. Copy the component gross onto Monthly Wage makes them match in one click.
  • Comp Off — a button at the top that opens this person's compensatory offs.
  • Devices tab — their registered phone and PIN.
  • Employment and Detail Fields tabs — confirmation date, notice period, past jobs, qualifications and other details you switched on in Field Settings.

9Help documents

Manage the guides people see under the Help menu.

Open Attendances → Configuration → Help Documents. Each record is one card, with a title, a short description and an icon. Upload a PDF, write the guide as HTML, or both. Open guide shows the HTML, and Open in PDF doc opens the PDF. Untick Active to hide a card.

Part 2 — Everyday Use

The screens HR and managers use every day. Employees mostly use the 369 Attendance app; whatever they do there shows up on these screens.

1Checking in and out

How a check-in gets into the system, and the few rules it has to follow.

A check-in can come from the 369 Attendance app, from the Odoo kiosk, or be typed in by HR on the attendance form. All of them create the same attendance record, and the day is graded the moment it arrives.

  • Once a day — after someone checks out, they can't check in again the same day. They must wait until tomorrow.
  • Days off — on a Sunday or holiday, the app first asks them to tap I am working today. Then check-in works as normal.
  • Late reason — after a late check-in, the app asks for a reason. HR entering a late check-in in Odoo must type one before saving.
  • Work from home — on a day with approved work from home, the check-in is marked Work From Home by itself. There's no special button.
  • Times — check-in times on every screen are shown in office time, so a person working from another city still looks right.
Watch out: If HR checks someone out by mistake, they cannot check in again that day. Check the name before you press Check Out Now.

2Late Records

A list of everyone who came in late, how late they were, and why.

Open Attendance Status → Late Records. Each row is one late check-in, with the time in office time, the Late Time, the Late Reason and the Work Location.

  • Filters — Late Only, On Time, Has Reason and Missing Reason. Group by Employee or Month to see who is often late.
  • Enter Late Reason — on the form, opens a small box to type or change the reason.
  • Check Out Now — checks out someone who forgot to. It asks you to confirm first.
  • Late Attendance Report — tick the rows you want, then Print → Late Attendance Report. You get a PDF with the expected time, how late, and the reason.
Good to know: Being late is recorded and shows in reports, but it never cuts pay. Only a half day does.

3Day Status

One line per person per day, saying what kind of day it was.

Open Attendance Status → Day Status. It shows this month by default. The colours make problems easy to spot:

4Absent Today

Who should be at work today but hasn't turned up.

Open Attendance Status → Absent Today. It lists everyone expected today who hasn't checked in. People on approved leave or an approved work-from-home day are left out.

  • When it fills up — the system checks every 30 minutes, starting once the Late Window Ends hour has passed. Early in the morning the list is empty; that's normal.
  • If they turn up later — once they check in, they drop off the list by themselves.
  • Marked absent by mistake? — approve their leave, or add their attendance, and the day is corrected.
Watch out: If Late Window Ends is left at 0, this list stays empty all day. Payroll still treats a past workday with no check-in and no leave as absent.

5Monthly Summary

A quick count of how often each person was late in a month.

Open Attendance Status → Monthly Summary. Pick the Month and Year. You can pick a Department or some Employees, or leave both empty for everyone. Press Generate Summary. You get one line per person with Total Late Days and Total Late Time.

  • Only the first check-in of each day counts.
  • Arriving after the late window is not counted as late.
  • Each run replaces the last one, so you can run it as often as you like.
Tip: Only HR Managers can create the summary. For anything to do with pay, use Employee Report → Generate Report instead.

6Asking for leave

How someone asks for time off, and how the system decides which days are paid.

Most people ask from the app, which shows how many days will be paid before they send it. In Odoo, open Leave Requests → My Leave Requests and press New:

  • Leave Type — Sick, Casual, Annual, Personal, Emergency, Compensatory Off, or Other. All types except Compensatory Off come out of the same paid allowance.
  • From Date and To Date — for one day, leave To Date empty. Tick Half Day for half of the first day.
  • Reason — required.

Press Submit for Approval. The request becomes Pending Approval, and the form shows:

FigureWhat it means
Number of DaysOnly workdays are counted. A Sunday or holiday in the middle of the leave is free.
Paid DaysAs many days as the yearly and monthly allowance both still allow.
Unpaid DaysThe rest. These are Loss of Pay.
Deduction AmountUnpaid days × one day's pay in that month.
Example: a leave over a weekend

Priya asks for Friday to Tuesday. Sunday is a weekly off and Monday is a public holiday. Only 3 days are counted: Friday, Saturday and Tuesday. With 1 paid day left this month, 1 day is paid and 2 are unpaid. With a daily rate of 1,000, the deduction is 2,000.

If Email HR on Submission is on in the Leave Policy, HR gets an email for each new request. It shows the employee, department, leave type, dates, paid and unpaid days, and the reason, with a Review and approve button.

Watch out: A request is refused if its dates overlap another request that isn't rejected or cancelled. That includes an unsent draft. It is also refused if every day in it is already a day off.

7Approving leave

Where managers say yes or no, and handle requests to cancel approved leave.

Open Leave Requests → All Leave Requests. It opens on the Pending filter, so you only see requests waiting for you. Open one, check the dates, the reason and how many days are paid, then press Approve or Reject. Approved leave shows as Leave in Day Status straight away.

Cancelling approved leave. Once leave is approved, the employee can't just cancel it. From the app they send a cancellation request with a reason, and the leave stays approved until HR decides. Pick the Cancellation Requested filter to find these, open one, and choose:

  • Approve Cancellation — the leave is cancelled and the days go back to their balance.
  • Keep Leave — the leave stays. Type why; the employee sees your reason in the app.
  • Cancel Leave — a manager can also cancel approved leave directly, without a request.
Watch out: Leave can't be cancelled once payroll for those dates is confirmed or paid. Put the payroll run back to draft first. Also, cancelling leave in the past turns those days into Absent if the person didn't check in.
Tip: To give a reason when rejecting, reject from the app. The Odoo Reject button rejects without asking for one.

8Approved Leaves Report

Every leave that has been approved, in one place.

Open Leave Requests → Approved Leaves Report. It is grouped by employee. Use the date filters, or group by month, to see who was off when. The paid and unpaid days here are exactly what payroll uses. Requests still waiting for a decision are in All Leave Requests, not here.

9Compensatory Off

A day off given back to people who worked on a Sunday or a holiday.

1. Say soTap "I am working today" in the app
→
2. WorkCheck in and out as usual
→
3. EarnA full day earns 1; a short day earns ½
→
4. Use itAsk for leave of type Compensatory Off

Open Leave Requests → Compensatory Off to see every credit. A day counts as a full day if the person worked at least Daily Paid Hours × Half Day Below Ratio. If the ratio is 0, it's half the Daily Paid Hours instead. When they use it, the oldest credit is used first, so nothing expires by accident.

Example

Daily Paid Hours is 8 and the ratio is 0.5, so a full day needs 4 hours. Priya works Sunday 5 January from 9:00 to 15:00, which is 6 hours: that earns 1 day. Arun works the same Sunday for 3 hours, which earns ½ day. With credits set to expire after 60 days, Priya's credit must be used by 6 March. Priya asks for Compensatory Off on 20 January, and once it's approved the credit shows as used.

On a credit, HR can press:

  • Grant — when someone said they were working but never checked out. Set Days Earned to ½ first if it was a short day.
  • Cancel Credit — for a credit that shouldn't have been given. Not possible once leave has used it.
  • Restore — brings back a cancelled or expired credit, with a fresh expiry date.
Good to know: Look at Days Left rather than the status. On 1 January, unused credits from last year move over, newest first, up to the limit in the Leave Policy. The rest expire. When a credit expires, only the unused part is lost.

10Leave Balances

How much paid leave and compensatory off each person has left this year.

Open Leave Requests → Leave Balances. There is one row per employee:

  • Allowed, Leave Taken and Paid Leave Left — their paid leave, from the Leave Policy.
  • Comp Off Earned, Comp Off Used and Comp Off Left — kept separately. Taking a compensatory off doesn't touch their paid leave.
  • Credits — opens that person's compensatory offs.

These numbers are worked out fresh each time you open the screen, so you can't sort or filter by them.

11Work From Home

Ask for a day at home, and have that day's check-in recorded as working from home.

Employees ask from the app, or in Odoo under Work From Home → My WFH Requests: press New, pick the WFH Date, give a reason and press Submit for Approval. Managers open All WFH Requests, which opens on the Pending filter, and press Approve.

  • No special check-in — on the approved day, the person checks in as usual and it's marked Work From Home. The request then shows Checked In and later Checked Out.
  • Same rules — a work-from-home day is graded like an office day. Starting late is Late, and not checking in is Absent.
  • Rejecting — done from the app, where the manager types a reason. The Odoo form has Approve and Cancel only.

12Help

Open the user guides from inside Odoo.

The Help menu opens Help & User Guide, with a card for each guide. Press Open guide to read it in a new tab. From there, Open in PDF doc gives the printable version. This manual is one of the cards.

Part 3 — Payroll, Reports & Access

Month-end work for HR Managers. Payroll and the monthly report both read the same day statuses and leave, so they always agree with the screens in Part 2.

1How pay is worked out

The few simple rules that turn a month of attendance into a salary.

Pay is built up from days. One day's pay is the Monthly Wage divided by that month's working days. Present days, late days, paid leave, Sundays and holidays are all paid in full. Three things reduce pay: an absent day (not paid), an unpaid leave day (not paid), and a half day (half paid).

Example: Priya's month

Monthly Wage 26,000. The month has 25 working days after one holiday, so one day's pay is 1,040.

What happenedDaysEffect on pay
Present, including 3 late days20Paid in full. Late costs nothing.
Half day1520 cut (half a day)
Paid leave1Paid in full
Unpaid leave22,080 not paid
Absent11,040 not paid
Sundays and the holiday—Always paid

Loss of Pay is 3.5 days (1 absent + ½ half day + 2 unpaid), which is 3,640. Priya is paid 26,000 − 3,640 = 22,360, before deductions like PF.

Create the month's payslips for everyone in a few clicks.

Open Payroll → Payroll Runs, press New, choose the Month and Year, and save. There is one run per company per month.

3Payslips

One employee's pay for one month, with the days behind it.

Open Payroll → Payslips. Payslips are made by the payroll run; you don't type them in. Each one shows every earning and deduction, and the days behind the pay. Press Print Payslip for the PDF.

4Generate Report

A monthly attendance and pay report for everyone, ready to download.

Open Employee Report → Generate Report. Pick the Month, Year and Company, then All Employees or Selected Employees. You can also narrow it to one Department. Press Generate Report.

The report has two tabs:

  • Employee Summary — one line per person: working days, present days, late days and time, paid and unpaid leave, compensatory off, deductions and the Final Amount. The buttons on each line show that person's days, late arrivals or leave.
  • Day-wise Details — every day for every person, with times, late minutes and leave.

At the top, ⟳ Refresh recalculates from the latest data. ⇩ PDF Report and ⇩ Excel Export download it. The Excel file has a summary sheet and a detailed sheet.

Watch out: Refresh uses today's Leave Policy. If you've changed the policy since that month, an old report can change when you refresh it.

5Past Reports

Every report you have made, kept for later.

Open Employee Report → Past Reports. Each row is one saved report, with its dates, total deductions and final amount. Open one to look at it again, or press Refresh inside it to update it. For a new month, use Generate Report. Only HR can see saved reports; for an employee this list is empty.

6What happens automatically

Jobs the system runs by itself, and the only email it sends.

JobHow oftenWhat it does
Mark absenteesEvery 30 minutesAfter the late window, marks anyone who hasn't checked in as Absent, or as Leave if they have approved leave.
Auto-approve requestsEvery 10 minutesApproves leave and work-from-home requests that waited longer than the Auto-Approval time.
Expire compensatory offsOnce a dayExpires overdue credits, clears "working today" declarations that never happened, and on 1 January carries credits into the new year.

The only email is the new leave request alert to HR, if it is switched on in the Leave Policy. Approvals, rejections and cancellations aren't emailed; employees see the result in the app.

Month-end checklist

  1. Open All Leave Requests and decide every pending request and cancellation request for the month.
  2. Open Day Status with the Absent filter. Anyone wrongly absent needs their leave approved or attendance added.
  3. Check Compensatory Off for Declared credits, and press Grant where the check-out was missed.
  4. Make sure every employee has a Monthly Wage, and that their salary parts add up to it.
  5. Create the Payroll Run, press Generate Payslips, fix any mismatch, then Confirm.
  6. Run Generate Report for the month and download the PDF or Excel for your records.
  7. Once salaries are paid, press Mark Paid and print the payslips.

Quick tips

  • Late costs nothing. A half day costs half a day. An absent day just isn't paid.
  • Late minutes count from the start time, not from the end of the grace minutes.
  • Leave is paid only while both the yearly and the monthly allowance have room.
  • Sundays and holidays inside a leave are free.
  • Save a Leave Policy before anyone asks for leave; without one, all leave is unpaid.
  • Employees can't cancel approved leave themselves. HR decides.
  • Set Late Window Ends, or nobody is ever marked absent during the day.
  • Salary parts must add up to the Monthly Wage before payroll can be confirmed.
  • A compensatory off is separate from paid leave, and the oldest one is used first.
