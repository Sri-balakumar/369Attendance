## About This Guide

This is the guide for the people who approve requests in 369 Attendance, from Alphalize Technologies: leave, cancellations of leave that is already approved, and work-from-home days. It covers the **HR** tab, the middle tab along the bottom of the screen, and everything you can reach from it.

You get the **HR** tab when your administrator has given you at least one approval permission (Leave Manager, WFH Manager, HR Officer or HR Manager) but not full settings access. Administrators see a **Config** tab in the same place instead, and staff with none of these permissions see only **Home** and **Profile**.

It describes the app exactly as it behaves on the phone. Every button name, field label and message in this guide is quoted from the app itself, so what you read here is what you will see on the screen.

Your own day works exactly as it does for everyone else. Checking in and out, applying for leave and asking to work from home are in the Employee Guide. Office hours, public holidays, the leave policy, auto-approval and payroll are set by your administrator and are in the Administrator Guide; none of them can be reached from the **HR** tab.

> TIP  Your HR page may not show every section in this guide. Each section appears only when your login holds the matching permission, and a missing section does not grey out. It is simply not there. Nothing is broken; ask your administrator.

## 369 Attendance at a Glance

- **Start of the day** — open **HR** and look for red numbers beside **Leave requests**, **Cancellation requests** and **Work from home requests**.
- **Through the day** — open each waiting request, read the figures, and **Approve** or **Reject** it.
- **When someone changes their mind** — decide their request with **Approve cancellation** or **Keep leave**.
- **Any time** — tap **Present**, **Late**, **On leave** or **Absent** under **TODAY** to see who is where.
- **Before you finish** — pull the HR page down to refresh it and check that nothing is still waiting.

# PART 1   YOUR HR PAGE

### Step 1  Find the HR Tab

Sign in as usual. Along the bottom of the screen sit three tabs: **Home**, **HR** and **Profile**. The tab you are on is filled in amber.

The **HR** tab can take a moment to appear after you sign in. The app asks the server what your login is allowed to do and adds the tab once the answer arrives. It asks each time you sign in or open the app, so if your administrator changes your permissions, sign out and back in to see the change.

**Home** is your own day: your check-in card, your month, and the shortcuts to your own leave and work from home. **Profile** is your own details. Both work the same for you as for any employee.

| What you tap | What happens |
| **Home** | Your own attendance, exactly as every employee sees it |
| **HR** | Today's attendance and the requests waiting for you |
| **Profile** | Your own details |

| IMAGE 1 | The HR page — the Your HR page guide, the APPROVALS rows with their red counts, and the TODAY boxes

### Step 2  Read the HR Page

The header reads **HR** with today's date beneath it. Below it is a yellow box headed **Your HR page**. It starts folded; tap it to open four short reminders, and tap it again to fold it away.

Under the box are two sections. **APPROVALS** has one row for each queue you can decide. **TODAY** shows who is in.

A red number on a row is how many requests are waiting in that queue. When nothing is waiting there is no number at all, so a row without one simply means there is nothing to do.

Pull the page down to refresh it. It also refreshes by itself every time you come back to it, so a request you have just decided has already left the count on your way back.

| What you see | What it means |
| **Leave requests** — **Approve or reject** | Leave waiting for its first decision |
| **Cancellation requests** — **Approved leave someone wants to cancel** | Approved leave the employee has asked HR to cancel |
| **Work from home requests** — **Approve or reject** | Work-from-home days waiting for a decision |
| Red number | How many are waiting in that queue |

| IMAGE 2 | The Your HR page guide opened, showing its four reminders

### Step 3  Know Why a Section Is Missing

The HR page is built from your permissions, and each part is checked on its own. Leave rights and work-from-home rights are two separate permissions: holding one does not give you the other.

| Your administrator has given you | You see |
| Leave Manager | **Leave requests** and **Cancellation requests** |
| WFH Manager | **Work from home requests** |
| HR Officer or HR Manager | **TODAY** and its four boxes |

If the page shows only **Checking what you can see…**, the app is still waiting for the server to answer, or none of the sections apply to your login.

> IMPORTANT  The server checks your permission again every time you approve or reject. If a permission is taken away while the app is open, a button may still be on screen, but the server refuses with a message such as **Only leave managers/admins can approve leave requests.**

# PART 2   LEAVE APPROVALS

### Step 4  Open the Leave Queue

Tap **Leave requests** on the HR page. **All Leave Requests** opens, subtitled **Everyone's requests**, already on the **Pending** chip.

The chips in the header change what the list shows. Above the cards a line counts what you are looking at, for example **2 requests**.

| Chip | What it shows |
| **Pending** | Requests waiting for a decision — the ones you act on |
| **Cancellation** | Approved leave the employee has asked to cancel |
| **All** | Every request, whatever its state |
| **Approved** | Leave already granted |
| **Rejected** | Leave already turned down |

With nothing waiting, **Pending** shows **Nothing waiting** and **Every request has been decided. New ones appear here as they are submitted.** Any other chip with nothing in it says **Nothing matches that filter this time.**

> TIP  The yellow **How to approve leave** box at the top is this step in four lines. Tap its heading to fold it away.

| IMAGE 5 | All Leave Requests on the Pending chip, with the How to approve leave guide and two waiting requests

### Step 5  Read a Request Card

Each card is one request, sorted by the first day of leave with the latest at the top.

| On the card | What it tells you |
| First line | The employee's name |
| Second line | The leave type and its length, for example **Casual · 2 days** or **Comp Off · half day** |
| Third line | The day, or the first and last day, for example **5 Oct 2026 – 6 Oct 2026** |
| Status chip | **Pending**, **Approved**, **Rejected**, **Cancelled** or **Draft** |
| **Cancel asked** | The leave is approved, and the employee has asked for it to be cancelled |
| **auto** | Nobody decided it: the server approved it after it had waited as long as your administrator allows |

The leave types are **Sick**, **Casual**, **Annual**, **Personal**, **Emergency**, **Comp Off** and **Other**.

Tap any card to open it. When you come back, the list reloads, so a decision you have just made shows straight away.

> TIP  Each chip lists the most recent 200 requests. On a busy site, older history may not appear under **All**.

### Step 6  Open a Leave Request

The request opens with the employee's name as the title and **Leave request** beneath it. The yellow box is now **How to decide this request**.

The top card shows the leave type and its status chip, then the figures. Some rows appear only when they apply.

| Row | What it tells you |
| **Dates** | The day, or the first and last day |
| **Days** | How many days, or **Half day** |
| **Paid / unpaid** | How many of those days are covered by paid leave, and how many are unpaid |
| **Earned on** | **Comp Off** only — the day or days the time off was earned |
| **Comp-off balance** | **Comp Off** only — what the employee has left. It turns red when it is less than the days asked for |
| **Deduction** | Shown in red, only when the unpaid days carry a pay deduction |
| **Submitted** | When the employee sent it |
| **Approved by** or **Decided by** | Who decided it, or **Auto-approved**. Not shown while the request is pending |

Below the card, **REASON** shows what the employee wrote, or **No reason given.**

> IMPORTANT  Read **Paid / unpaid** before you approve. A figure such as **1 / 1** means one of the two days is not covered by paid leave. Whether an unpaid day costs pay depends on your leave policy; when it does, the **Deduction** row shows the amount.

| IMAGE 6 | A Comp Off request — Earned on and Comp-off balance, with Reject and Approve beneath

| IMAGE 7 | A two-day Casual request where one day is unpaid — Paid / unpaid reads 1 / 1

### Step 7  Approve a Leave Request

Tap **Approve**. A window asks **Approve this request?** and explains that the employee **will be marked on leave, and those days leave the attendance grading.** Tap **Approve** again to confirm, or **Cancel** to go back.

On success you see **Request approved.** and are returned to the queue, where the request has already left **Pending**.

| What you tap | What happens |
| **Approve** on the request | Opens the confirmation |
| **Approve** in the window | Grants the leave; the employee sees it as approved |
| **Cancel** | Closes the window; nothing changes |

If the server refuses, its own reason appears in a red message and the request stays open, reloaded to show its latest state. Two common causes are a **Comp Off** request with too little balance left to cover it, and a request that somebody else has just decided.

| IMAGE 8 | The Approve this request? window on a Comp Off request

### Step 8  Reject a Leave Request

Tap **Reject**. The window asks **Reject this request?** and reminds you that **The employee sees this reason, so make it useful.** Type into **Reason for rejection**, then tap **Reject**.

A reason is required. Tap **Reject** with the box empty and it turns red with **Give a reason — the employee sees this.** Nothing is sent until you type something. While the app is sending, the button reads **Working…**.

On success you see **Request rejected.** The reason stays on the request and appears under **REJECTED BECAUSE** whenever it is opened again.

| What you tap | What happens |
| **Reject** on the request | Opens the reason window |
| **Reason for rejection** | Type what the employee will read |
| **Reject** in the window | Turns the request down and records you as the person who decided it |
| **Cancel** | Closes the window and throws away what you typed |

| IMAGE 9 | The Reject this request? window with a reason typed in

### Step 9  Know What Cannot Be Changed

Only a pending request can be decided. Open a rejected or cancelled request and the buttons are replaced by a single line, such as **Already rejected — only a pending request can be decided.**

There is no way to undo a rejection or to change its reason afterwards. If a rejection was a mistake, the employee applies again. Approved leave is the one exception: it can still be cancelled, as PART 3 explains, and it shows a **Cancel this leave** button instead of the line.

If two of you open the same request and the other person decides first, your tap is refused with **Only pending requests can be approved.** or **Only pending requests can be rejected.**, and the screen reloads to show what they decided. The same happens if the server approved the request automatically while you were reading it.

| What you see | What it means |
| **Already rejected — only a pending request can be decided.** | Turned down; it stays that way |
| **Already cancelled — only a pending request can be decided.** | Withdrawn or cancelled; it stays that way |
| **REJECTED BECAUSE** | The reason given when it was rejected |
| **That request no longer exists.** | The request could not be found. Go back and pull the list down to refresh it |
| **The server refused that.** | The server said no without giving a reason. Try again, and ask your administrator if it keeps happening |

| IMAGE 10 | A rejected request — the REJECTED BECAUSE box and the line saying only a pending request can be decided

# PART 3   CANCELLATION REQUESTS

### Step 10  Open the Cancellation Queue

An employee cannot cancel leave that has already been approved. Instead they ask HR, with a reason, and those requests collect under **Cancellation requests** on the HR page.

Tapping it opens **All Leave Requests** on the **Cancellation** chip. Each card shows the leave as **Approved**, with an orange **Cancel asked** chip beneath.

Nothing has changed yet. The leave stays approved, and keeps counting as leave, until you decide.

| IMAGE 11 | The Cancellation chip — an approved leave carrying the Cancel asked chip

### Step 11  Read the Cancellation Request

Open the card. The yellow box now reads **How to decide a cancellation**. Below the usual figures and **REASON** sits an orange box headed **CANCELLATION REQUESTED**, with the employee's reason for cancelling and when they asked, for example **Asked 27 Sep, 15:25**.

**REASON** is why they wanted the leave in the first place. **CANCELLATION REQUESTED** is why they no longer want it.

| What you tap | What happens |
| **Approve cancellation** | Cancels the leave and gives the days back to the balance |
| **Keep leave** | Leaves it approved; you must say why |

| IMAGE 12 | A cancellation request — the orange CANCELLATION REQUESTED box, with Keep leave and Approve cancellation

### Step 12  Approve the Cancellation

Tap **Approve cancellation**. The window asks **Approve the cancellation?** and explains that the leave is cancelled and its days go back to the balance, then warns: **Refused if that month's payroll is already confirmed or paid.** Tap **Approve**, or **Back** to leave it as it is.

On success you see **Leave cancelled.** The leave now shows **Cancelled**, and it leaves the **Cancellation** chip. When the last one is dealt with, the chip reads **No cancellation requests**.

> IMPORTANT  Once the payroll run covering any day of the leave has been confirmed or paid, the server refuses with a message such as **Payroll for September 2026 is already confirmed, so this leave can no longer be cancelled. Reopen the payroll run first.** Payroll is not on the HR tab. Ask your administrator to reopen the run, then try again.

| IMAGE 13 | The Approve the cancellation? window, with its payroll warning

| IMAGE 14 | The Cancellation chip once every request has been decided

### Step 13  Keep the Leave

Tap **Keep leave**. The window asks **Keep this leave?** and explains **The leave stays approved. The employee sees your reason.** Type into **Why it stays**, then tap **Keep leave**.

The reason is required. An empty box turns red with **Give a reason — the employee sees this.**

On success you see **Cancellation declined; leave kept.** The **Cancel asked** chip disappears and the leave carries on as approved. If the employee's plans change again, they can send a fresh cancellation request.

| What you tap | What happens |
| **Why it stays** | Type what the employee will read |
| **Keep leave** in the window | Declines the cancellation; the leave stays approved |
| **Cancel** | Closes the window; the request is still waiting |

| IMAGE 15 | The Keep this leave? window with the reason typed in

### Step 14  Cancel Approved Leave Yourself

You do not have to wait for the employee to ask. Open any approved leave that has no cancellation request on it, from the **Approved** or **All** chip, and a single **Cancel this leave** button sits at the bottom.

Tapping it asks **Cancel this approved leave?**, with the same payroll warning. Tap **Cancel leave** to go ahead, or **Back** to keep it. The result is the same as approving a cancellation: **Leave cancelled.**, and the days go back to the balance.

This window asks for no reason, so tell the employee yourself why their leave was cancelled.

| What you tap | What happens |
| **Cancel this leave** | Opens the confirmation |
| **Cancel leave** | Cancels the approved leave |
| **Back** | Closes the window; the leave stays approved |

| IMAGE 16 | An approved leave with the Cancel this leave button, and the Cancel this approved leave? window

# PART 4   WORK FROM HOME APPROVALS

### Step 15  Open the Work From Home Queue

Tap **Work from home requests** on the HR page. **All WFH Requests** opens on the **Pending** chip, with the yellow box **How to approve work from home**.

Each card shows the employee, **Work from home** and the day asked for. There is no **Cancellation** chip here: an employee who no longer needs an approved day cancels it themselves.

| Chip | What it shows |
| **Pending** | Days waiting for a decision |
| **All** | Every request, whatever its state |
| **Approved** | Granted, and not yet checked in |
| **Done** | The employee checked in and out on the day |
| **Rejected** | Turned down |

Under **All** you may also see **Checked in** (working from home right now), **Cancelled** (withdrawn by the employee) and **Expired** (approved, but the day passed without a check-in).

| IMAGE 17 | All WFH Requests on the Pending chip, with one waiting request

### Step 16  Decide a Work From Home Request

Tap a card. The request opens with **Work from home** under the employee's name and shows **Date** and **Submitted**, then **Approved by** or **Decided by** once it has been decided. After the employee has checked in, a **Worked** row adds the check-in time and the hours worked. **REASON** is underneath.

Tap **Approve** and the window says the employee **will be able to check in from home** on that day. Tap **Approve** to confirm.

Tap **Reject** and you get the same **Reject this request?** window as for leave. A reason is required here too, and the server refuses an empty one on its own account with **Rejection reason is required**.

On the day, the employee checks in as usual and it is recorded as work from home. On the **TODAY** list they carry a **WFH** chip.

> IMPORTANT  An approved work-from-home day cannot be withdrawn from the HR tab. Opening it shows **Already approved — only a pending request can be decided.** Only the employee can cancel it.

# PART 5   TODAY AT A GLANCE

### Step 17  See Who Is In Today

The **TODAY** section needs the HR Officer or HR Manager permission. It shows four boxes, each with a count, and a list of names beneath the box that is selected. **Present** is selected when the page opens.

Tap a box to list the people in it. The list appears right there on the HR page; tapping a name does not open anything further.

| Box | Who it counts |
| **Present** | Everyone who turned up today, including those who were late or are on a half day |
| **Late** | Those who checked in after the grace time |
| **On leave** | Those covered by approved leave today |
| **Absent** | Those the server has marked absent |

Each row shows the person's name, today's date and a status chip: **Present**, **Late**, **Half Day**, **Leave** or **Absent**. A **WFH** chip means they are working from home, an **On leave** chip means approved leave covers the day, and **auto-stamped** means the server marked the day itself. No pay figures are shown here.

| If the box is empty you see | Why |
| **Nobody has checked in yet today.** | Nobody is in yet |
| **Nobody is late today.** | Everyone so far was on time |
| **Nobody is on leave today.** | No approved leave covers today |
| **Nobody is marked absent yet. Absent appears once the late window ends.** | It is too early for anyone to be marked absent |

| IMAGE 21 | TODAY with the Present box selected and the names listed beneath it

### Step 18  Know When Absent Fills In

A person gets a row for today once they check in, once approved leave covers the day, or once the server marks them absent. That last step happens only after the late window your administrator has set, so early in the morning **Absent** is normally empty.

**Absent** is therefore not a live list of who has not arrived yet. Before the late window ends, someone missing is simply not on any list.

If someone shows as **Absent** and you know they have leave waiting, check the **Pending** chip on **Leave requests**. Approving leave for a day already marked absent moves them to leave.

Someone working on their weekly off or on a public holiday is not counted in any of the four boxes.

> TIP  The HR tab shows today only. Past days, late records and monthly reports are on your administrator's screens, not here.

## Things to Remember

- **Only a pending request can be decided.** A rejection is final; approved leave can only be cancelled.
- **A reason is required to reject, and to keep leave.** The employee reads it, so make it useful.
- **Read Paid / unpaid before you approve.** It shows how much of the leave is unpaid.
- **Leave rights and work-from-home rights are separate.** Holding one does not give you the other.
- **Cancelling approved leave stops at payroll.** Once that month's payroll is confirmed or paid, the server refuses until your administrator reopens the run.
- **Cancel this leave asks for no reason.** Tell the employee yourself.
- **auto means nobody decided it.** The server approved it after the wait your administrator set.
- **Someone else may get there first.** If the server refuses, the screen reloads to show the decision already made.
- **An approved work-from-home day is the employee's to cancel**, not yours.
- **Absent fills in after the late window ends.** An empty Absent box early in the morning is normal.
- **Permission changes need a fresh sign-in.** Sign out and back in after your administrator changes your rights.
- **Rules, holidays, leave policy and payroll are not on the HR tab.** Ask your administrator.
